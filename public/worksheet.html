News Without Newsrooms · Common group worksheet

What could users see, verify, and do in the interface at this point?

1. Stage and decision

Post / spread / aftermath. Who is deciding what?

2. Record and observation

Identify the source. What does it actually show?

3. What users could see, verify, and do

Identify visible cues, information, and available actions. Separate availability from actual use.

4. Applicable practice or rule

What editorial practice, platform condition, or policy applies in this context?

5. Unknowns and disagreement

What cannot be established from this record? Where does the group disagree?

6. One research question

Connect a user decision to an interface or system condition.

7. Evidence and context

What would answer this question, and in which setting?

When you exchange worksheets

Propose an intervention at another stage or in another context. What should change for the user? What conditions must hold, what could fail, and how would you evaluate it?
